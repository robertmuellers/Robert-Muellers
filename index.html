Robert Müllers

Robert Müllers is a contemporary painter, based in Düsseldorf specializing in colourful, large-scale oil works.

(b. 1992) His work examines figurative abstraction, gesture, and physical texture. Müllers' paintings inhabit a space between quiet states and vivid expressionism, drawing upon raw brushwork juxtaposed with distinct linework.
